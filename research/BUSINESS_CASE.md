# Business case

A preventive-care lead must decide whether to contact everyone eligible, ration a channel by risk score, or use a validated benefit-based rule. At low digital contact costs and high eligibility, a uniform strategy is a practical comparator. If contacts are scarce, compare rules at identical capacity and show their added outcomes and uncertainty. Before changing operations, screen candidate policies against a meaningful minimum lift and a feasible randomized pilot size.

Study 4 estimates incremental vaccinations for assigned bundles. A channel planner can explore hypothetical unit costs, eligibility and benefits. Neither a budget scenario nor an influenza outcome supports a claim about realized savings, other preventive procedures, or the effect of nurse calls.
