The Haryana branch of the research addresses which assigned interventions increase preventive-care uptake and how to allocate outreach capacity across communities. It uses a household-facing reminder bundle and a community outcome. Treat the village-month endpoint as a count. The package cannot identify individual clinical-risk effects or separate texting from voice calls. Additional months are repeated measurements, and the reminder system partly failed after the authors’ analysis window. A monthly split alone does not supply independent external validation. Keep location keys, survey records and row-level files private. Publish only attributed, disclosure-reviewed aggregates.

## First replication check

Record a narrow reproduction of the published §4.5 information-hub coefficient and its reported pure-control baseline before estimating downstream policies. The authors report 1.89 additional measles vaccinations per village-month and a control mean of 7.32 in the ambassador-eligible sample. These published reference values were known before the independent run.

Use the archive’s pinned prepared file. Retain `first_implementation == 1` and `seedsrisk == 1`. Reproduce the population-weighted regression of the measles count on ten outreach-variant indicators with district-calendar-month fixed effects. Cluster by health sub-center. For the pure-control baseline, use rows assigned to control in all three intervention domains and weight by village population. Require both point estimates to agree with the published figures at two-decimal precision. Before using uncertainty or analyzing policy interactions, check the variance against an independent implementation and reconcile the relevant higher-level randomization and support. [Published Figure 4 and specification](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.3982/ECTA19739#ecta19739-sec-0014)
