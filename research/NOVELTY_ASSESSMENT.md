# Novelty assessment

No new causal estimator or first use of causal targeting is claimed. Prior work includes flu megastudy personalization, inference-aware policy selection and a prospective Geisinger trial. The previous review's suggestion that the risk-band pattern was unknown is too broad. It identified prior authors' analysis of that pattern and this project has not established that a later formal comparison remains novel.

A potential contribution is careful replication, defensible comparison under matched capacity, a transparent prospective pilot tool and study-specific channel economics. Whether a comparable temporal design and sufficiently powered risk-band analysis are possible remains open until protocols and data rights are resolved.
