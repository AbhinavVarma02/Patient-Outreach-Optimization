# Decisions

## D01 Governing scope
The pasted v1.0 contract supersedes the v0.2 review's build scope. Retain the integrated static application and trial document-intelligence workflows while using the review's advice on power, leakage, exclusions, dependence and estimation. Adopting the old static-report-only scope would omit explicit current requirements. Revisit scope only with a later owner instruction.

## D02 Rights and data boundaries
Preserve supplied originals in ignored local research storage. Track the byte-identical inventory as provenance metadata and an independent public-safe reconciliation. The source inventory has no recorded license. Hold row-level acquisition and analysis pending verified permission. Revisit with an applicable reuse license or written author confirmation.

## D03 Limited demonstration
Use a static workspace, deterministic Python research code and browser-local, clearly fictional calculations. Keep public source evidence separate and avoid backend state or user data. This permits independent work during the rights hold and avoids ongoing cloud compute. A reviewed evidence artifact may be added once the relevant scientific and rights gates pass.

## D04 Deployment and release
Deploy the vetted static build by direct upload to the existing Cloudflare account. Keep GitHub CI separate from deployment to make the publication gate explicit. Wrangler's default creation path delegated to Workers. Its direct Pages creation option created the intended Pages project. Releases remain labeled as demonstrations until the empirical and rights gates pass.

## D05 Approved interface

The project owner approved the v0.1.0-demo production design. Carry its typography, colors, spacing, navigation and layout forward. Add functionality and evidence through the existing patterns, with visible changes limited to functionality, accessibility or usability. The production screenshot references and comparison procedure are in [docs/UI_DESIGN.md](docs/UI_DESIGN.md). Revisit the baseline only after an intentional change is reviewed and verified in production.

## D06 Randomized outreach fallback

Keep the Geisinger protocol and rights gate in place. In parallel, use the CC BY 4.0 Haryana replication archive for an explicitly scoped, village-level study of immunization outreach. This is the closest inspected public candidate with verified randomization, usable aggregate baseline and outcome data, and a clear license. Its interventions and population differ from Geisinger. Begin with the published reference check and require design and disclosure review before wider use. [Feasibility evidence](research/DATASET_FEASIBILITY.md)

## D07 Community analysis and release boundary

Ship the independently verified Figure 4 replication, PHC-clustered sensitivity and transparent exploratory matched-block holdout. Restrict learned ranking to a fixed ridge T-learner and compare with uniform, baseline uptake and predicted control outcomes. The data support a narrow village decision. Sparse cross-randomized cells, small clustered evaluation support and interference rule out a full factorial optimizer and patient extrapolation. Label the nominal intervals and their calibration limits in the interface. Pursue the separately licensed Sierra Leone design for household-level outreach replication. [Haryana study card](docs/HARYANA_RESEARCH.md) · [Alternative dataset review](research/DATASET_FEASIBILITY.md#companion-and-alternative-datasets)

## D08 Extraction delivery

Keep reviewed study evidence as the release path. The 30-trial benchmark gave neither local generation nor its verifier a reliable coverage gain. Keep labels, sources and results frozen for reference. Test future changes on a new holdout after development, with second review for ambiguous unit and timing labels. [Benchmark](docs/AI_EVALUATION.md)

## D09 Sierra Leone estimands

Publish the replicated adult uptake contrasts for assignment to the combined mobile package and the incremental structure visit. Pooled mobile assignment has known 2/3 propensity, with each package version assigned once per triplet. The released structure roster does not reconcile with the stated visit cap everywhere, so preserve the published within-village replication and limit experimental targeting to the village lottery. Use fixed ridge learners in a triplet-disjoint internal holdout. Revisit structure targeting after denominator and interference resolution. [Study card](docs/SIERRA_LEONE_RESEARCH.md)

## D10 Conservative small-sample inference

Use assignment-aware bounded intervals as the principal Sierra Leone policy uncertainty. Verify score and range calculations by exact enumeration and calibration. In Haryana keep the original nominal intervals and add finite-sample inference for a capped-count sensitivity. The bounded intervals trade precision for finite-sample control under documented lottery and independence assumptions. They do not repair bias due to interference or observation. [Inference validation](docs/MODEL_VALIDATION.md)

## D11 Extraction gate after repair

Use the original benchmark for diagnostics and the disjoint 30-trial evaluation to assess the frozen system. The best deterministic path scored 14 of 30 and the verified model scored 3 of 30. Keep reviewed sources in production. Additional OCR, vocabulary, context and verification work stays experimental and requires a successor holdout for any new quality claim. [Evaluation](docs/AI_EVALUATION.md)
