# Technical guide

The project evaluates whether outreach decisions should rely on clinical risk, expected benefit or a simpler uniform policy. A risk score forecasts an outcome. A causal policy needs evidence about the difference induced by an assigned action. The pilot tool sizes a prospective experiment in which participants are randomized to two policies and capacity is matched across arms.

1. Read the [literature](../research/LITERATURE_REVIEW.md), [study cards](DATA_CARD.md) and [protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) for population, endpoint and estimand choices.
2. Check [licensing](LICENSING.md) before acquisition. Use the pinned public-metadata check whenever preparing a replication run.
3. Run [reproducibility commands](REPRODUCIBILITY.md) to evaluate estimators and frozen policies on fictional known truth.
4. Review [model validation](MODEL_VALIDATION.md) and [document extraction evaluation](AI_EVALUATION.md) before interpreting the reports.
5. Use the [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [UI](UI_DESIGN.md) and [release safety](PUBLICATION_POLICY.md) guides to build and deploy the reviewed static artifact.

Known-propensity AIPW uses independent nuisance models and paired scores to compare fixed policies on the same outcomes. A richer causal forest or network would require defensible feature sets, sufficient real data and model-selection safeguards. Fractional ties preserve expected matched capacity. The planner uses direct arithmetic to find feasible single-channel allocations because the objective and constraints are separable within each candidate. Treatment mixtures and eligibility-specific effects need additional design support.

## Empirical pipeline

The [Haryana track](HARYANA_RESEARCH.md) starts with a verifiable deposit and a pretreatment feature contract. It validates a one-hot assignment within each intervention domain, nests village assignments in health sub-centers and PHCs, and gates downstream analysis on two published reference numbers. The policy experiment fits ridge per treatment on 14 whole training PHCs, freezes the fit and ranks a disjoint roster. Paired augmented scores reuse the documented conditional block lottery. Cluster sensitivity, a fixed-roster calibration and a conditional sharp-null test expose limitations of the small test sample. A separate export script reduces the experiment to selected rounded aggregates. [Architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) · [Protocol](../research/HARYANA_PROTOCOL.md)

The [document benchmark](AI_EVALUATION.md) compares exact scorable labels and abstention across registry metadata, deterministic PDF text parsing, installed local inference and an evidence verifier on the same pinned inputs. Local generation only improves a feature when the returned data are demonstrably supported and useful. The benchmark did not meet this standard.

The [Sierra Leone pipeline](SIERRA_LEONE_RESEARCH.md) verifies original data and analysis materials before running an independent published regression replication. Its policy run fits T- and DR-learners on whole training triplets and keeps frozen rules, roster and fit digests in the run manifest. It evaluates the known 2/3 assignment probability on disjoint triplets and publishes only reviewed aggregate results. The finite-sample bounds quantify how little is learned about gains at matched capacity. The structure-level replication alone leaves key support and interference questions for a visit allocation policy.

## US payer integration

Medicare Advantage quality teams can use contract reporting to identify a preventive-care outcome worth investigating. CMS measures are contract observations with different denominators and windows. The planner uses the rounded CMS average as a reference for a hypothetical, fixed plan denominator. It computes whole-contact limits from feasible reach, operational capacity and campaign budget, then calculates the absolute completion lift a pilot would have to demonstrate. A zero-reach scenario leaves a positive gap without a defined lift. A gap larger than reach is infeasible even if every contact produces an additional completion. No Stars or bonus prediction follows from this calculation.

For pilot design, compare a defined outreach bundle with usual care over the same time window and predeclare how outcome recording, use of external services and missingness are handled. The UCLA flu reminder result and the SCORE screening bundle provide distinct examples. Decisions about fit, timing and cost remain local. Neither the US trial summaries nor the community effects seed planning inputs. ## Calculation and decision references

For assignment `A`, outcome `Y`, known treatment probability `e` and independent outcome predictions `m0` and `m1`, the augmented effect score is `m1 - m0 + A (Y - m1) / e - (1 - A) (Y - m0) / (1 - e)`. Multiply it by the difference in outreach probability between two fixed policies to estimate a paired gain. Average on the declared outcome unit and respect the randomized blocks in inference. In Sierra Leone the DR learner uses out-of-fold scores in the training triplets and the final comparison uses separate test triplets. Haryana uses a conditional matched-block design, PHC holdout, nominal t intervals and a labeled capped-count sensitivity. Wide intervals and alternative unadjusted estimates favor the simpler uniform allocation in these settings. [Statistical validation](MODEL_VALIDATION.md) · [Research risk](MODEL_RISK_ASSESSMENT.md)

The payer workflow needs `max(0, ceil(eligible × benchmark) − expected completions)` new completions. Capacity is the minimum of reach, operational limit and the whole contacts that can be afforded. Dividing the gap by feasible reach gives a required increment. Values above one flag infeasibility. The pilot treats the assumed population-scale rate difference as the fixed contrast and evaluates two-sided power with a normal approximation, cluster design effect and attrition inflation. The minimum detectable difference holds the modeled variance fixed. [Planning scenario](US_PAYER_EVIDENCE.md) · [Power analysis](../research/POWER_ANALYSIS.md)
