# Allocation and fairness

The fictional examples use coarse clinical-risk bands. They support inspection of allocation logic and do not measure disparity in any real population. Contactability may differ by channel and group. The channel planner's eligibility fraction is an explicit scenario input.

A future real evaluation should compare allocation, effects and uncertainty for eligible subgroups. Assignments must use pre-treatment covariates. Report where subgroup-arm cells cannot be disclosed at the threshold of 11 or where complementary suppression is required. Preserve demographic fields solely for justified validation unless the approved protocol explicitly permits their use in a policy. Unreported strata remain unassessed.

The Haryana rule uses village literacy and vaccination-history shares. Literacy can proxy for structural inequity. The analysis excluded caste and religion and did not measure individual or group-level distributional impact. Its sparse arm and subgroup overlap constrains public fairness comparisons.
