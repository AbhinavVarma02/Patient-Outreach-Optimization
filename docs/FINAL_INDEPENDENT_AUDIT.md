# Final critical review

This review challenged the release after implementation from statistical, payer, ML, AI and software perspectives. The implementing agent performed the review in a separate pass. It has no independent human reviewer or external clinical validation. The report evaluates a public research demo.

## Final decision

The portfolio can claim independent reproduction of published study arithmetic, internally held-out community comparisons, reviewed US aggregate evidence, a browser-local scenario workspace and bounded extraction evaluation. Keep the authorized causal results and all negative comparisons in the record. The original empirical contract stays open on Geisinger rights and replication, patient-level causal and channel effects, comparable external or temporal validation, and AI release accuracy. Defer those extensions. [Requirements matrix](REQUIREMENTS_TRACEABILITY.md) · [Current production evidence](DEPLOYMENT.md)
