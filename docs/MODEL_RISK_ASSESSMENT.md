# Model risk assessment

## Intended decision

Analysts can explore hypothetical outreach allocations, campaign feasibility and pilot sizing. Researchers can reproduce licensed community study contrasts and compare fixed village policies. Local model weights and predictions stay in the research environment. Public calculations and reviewed study summaries support human review.

| Risk | Control and decision rule | Residual limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Patient-level use | No patient identifiers, outreach execution, individually scored outputs or published model parameters. | No clinical, regulatory, security certification or deployment authorization for healthcare operations. |
| Data integrity and provenance | Verify original source bytes, manifests, feature ranges and assignment checks. Record protocol versions and fit digests. Verify exact published rounding before policies. | Source completeness and program observation differ by trial. Geisinger participant reuse and payload integrity remain unresolved. |
| Policy selection | Predeclare frozen learners, hold out whole PHCs or triplets and compare at matched capacity. Keep the simpler uniform baseline unless a valid evaluation establishes practical, reliable gain. | Geographic holdouts reuse each study's setting and design. The 19 Haryana test PHCs and 20 Sierra Leone test triplets limit precision. |
| Population transfer | Identify the assignment, endpoint, denominator and follow-up next to any effect. Keep US payer assumptions separate from community effects. | Compatibility with a later trial or payer cohort remains unverified. |
| Inequitable reach or benefit | Include channel eligibility in scenarios. In a prospective pilot, predefine protected subgroup checks for offer, delivery, completion and missingness. Use the publication threshold of at least 11 plus complementary suppression. | The small community holdouts and geography-level features cannot establish fairness for demographic groups. Village literacy and baseline uptake can reflect structural differences. |
| Interference and missingness | Preserve assignment blocks, compare outcome windows and simulate spillover. Structure targeting stays gated until eligibility, draw and observation rules can be reconstructed. | Cross-village spillover can bias gains. Bounded intervals address sampling assumptions and do not remove this bias. |
| Document extraction reliability | Public cards come from reviewed sources. Local extraction abstains on unresolved support and must clear its original accuracy and abstention gates before reconsideration. | Page-conditioned benchmarks and single-reviewer labels leave layout, OCR, alternate valid spans and external annotation unvalidated. |
| Privacy and deployment | Serve a verified five-file build with aggregate allowlists, restricted network policy and local theme preference only. | Public static hosting has no authorized workflow for regulated patient records or local reidentification. |

Before any actual intervention, require human ownership of eligibility, channel constraints, costs, patient access, outcome definition, adequate power and a preregistered analysis. Track assignment, receipt and completion separately. Re-open learned policy evaluation with a compatible independent rollout.
