## Scientific dispositions

- **Scientifically unsupported.** Treat a mobile clinic and mobilization package as a bundled intervention. Avoid causal cross-trial rankings, imputing an assignment denominator from the observed Sierra Leone roster, or converting camp counts into patient vaccinations. No nominal estimate establishes a policy gain over uniform at the studied capacity.
- **Externally blocked.** No compatible, licensed US participant-level dataset or external Sierra Leone rollout was verified. Geisinger requires substantive reuse authorization. Structure targeting requires the preassignment eligibility and draw history plus outcome observation and interference rules. [Final data search](../research/DATASET_FEASIBILITY.md#final-bounded-us-and-sierra-leone-check)
- **No longer necessary for this demo.** A live model API, orchestration service or paid inference infrastructure adds no demonstrated decision value. Existing reviewed sources and static calculators support the released workflows. This disposition does not waive original scientific acceptance criteria.
